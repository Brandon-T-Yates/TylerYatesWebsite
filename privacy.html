Site Info & Privacy

A plain-language explanation of how this personal portfolio is hosted, what optional analytics can measure, and how to control your preference.

Last updated: September 10, 2026

Personal site and employer information

This is my personal portfolio. The views and opinions shared here are my own and do not represent my current or former employers. Company names, product names, logos, and trademarks referenced on this site belong to their respective owners.

Projects and case studies are based on my own work, learning, or publicly shareable information. No confidential or proprietary employer information is intentionally published.

Optional Google Analytics

Google Analytics 4 is used only if you explicitly choose “Allow Analytics.” Before that choice—or after you disable analytics—the Google Analytics script is not loaded and portfolio events are not sent to Google.

If allowed, Google Analytics may measure aggregate information such as visits, pages viewed, approximate location, browser or device details, and selected portfolio interactions. It may use first-party cookies, including _ga cookies, to distinguish users and sessions. This site disables advertising storage, Google signals, and advertising personalization in its analytics configuration.

Learn more from Google’s GA4 data collection information.

Browser and local storage

This site uses your browser’s localStorage only to remember your analytics preference across pages. The key is tyler_portfolio_analytics_consent and its value is either granted or denied. Normal browser caching may also occur when site files are loaded.

GitHub Pages hosting

This static site is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub states that a visitor’s IP address is logged and stored for security purposes when a GitHub Pages site is visited, whether or not the visitor is signed in. That processing is handled by GitHub under its own privacy practices.

See GitHub’s GitHub Pages data collection information.

External links

Links to other websites, downloads, or services may take you outside this portfolio. Those destinations operate under their own privacy policies and terms, which I do not control.

No sale or targeted advertising

I do not sell personal information collected through this portfolio, and I do not use this site to serve targeted advertising. Analytics is used only to understand aggregate site traffic and portfolio interactions when a visitor allows it.

Children’s privacy

This portfolio is intended for a general professional audience and is not directed to children under 13. I do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children through accounts or submission forms on this site. If you believe a child has provided personal information through this site, please contact me.

Privacy choices

You can allow or disable analytics at any time. Disabling analytics stops future portfolio analytics events and removes site-set Google Analytics cookies where the browser permits it.

Analytics preference: not selected
