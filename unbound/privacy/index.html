Unbound Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes the Unbound mobile application. Unbound is designed to work without accounts, subscriptions, advertising, analytics, or tracking.

Information Unbound collects

Unbound does not automatically collect or transmit personal information to Unbound-operated servers. Unbound currently has no backend or cloud service for receiving app usage information.

Information stored on your device

Unbound stores limited information locally on your device needed for the app to function, including:

  • onboarding preferences
  • acknowledgment preferences
  • restriction or lock state
  • authorization state
  • widget state
  • Apple-provided app-selection tokens

These data are used to provide app functionality.

App selection information

App-selection identities and tokens used by Apple's Family Controls system remain within the native iOS implementation. They are not sent to an Unbound server. Flutter receives only limited state, such as authorization status and app counts, rather than the underlying app-selection tokens.

Screen Time and Apple frameworks

Unbound uses Apple technologies including:

  • Screen Time
  • Family Controls
  • ManagedSettings
  • WidgetKit

These system frameworks provide the restriction functionality used by Unbound.

Analytics, advertising, and tracking

Unbound does not currently use:

  • analytics SDKs
  • advertising SDKs
  • tracking technologies
  • crash-reporting services that transmit user information
  • third-party behavioral analytics

Accounts and payments

Unbound does not require a user account. Unbound does not currently offer subscriptions or in-app purchases.

Support email

If you choose to contact support@tyleryates.me, we may receive:

  • your email address
  • the contents of your message
  • any information you voluntarily include

This information is used to respond to the support request. The Unbound app itself does not automatically send this information.

Children

Unbound does not knowingly collect personal data through the app because the app does not operate a system for collecting user information.

Changes to this policy

This page may be updated if Unbound's features or privacy practices change. The “Last updated” date above will reflect any revisions.

Contact

Questions about Unbound or this policy: support@tyleryates.me
