Unbound

Take back your time.

Choose the apps you want to keep. When you're ready to focus, lock everything else away.

Coming to the App Store

The idea

A different way to think about app blocking.

Most app blockers start by asking what you want to block.

Unbound flips that around.

Choose the apps you want to keep available, then lock everything else away when it's time to focus.

How it works

Three small steps.

  1. Choose what stays

    Pick the apps you want available.

  2. →

  3. Lock in

    Start focused time with one tap.

  4. →

  5. Unlock when you're ready

    Return to normal when you're finished.

The product

Built to stay out of the way.

Product principles

Simple by design.

No account · No subscription · No tracking · No streaks

Unbound is intentionally small. No dashboards, gamification, or complicated setup — just a quick way to make room for what matters.
