Unbound

Take back your time.

Why I built it

I kept coming back to a simple problem: I knew what I wanted to be doing, but distraction was always one tap away. Most app blockers start by asking what you want to block. I wanted to flip that around — choose what actually matters, then make everything else harder to reach.

Product choices

No account
There’s nothing Unbound needs an account for. Your settings stay on your device.
No subscription
The core focus experience shouldn’t depend on another recurring payment.
No streaks or gamification
I didn’t want to build another app competing for your attention.
One-button focus
Choose what stays once, then LOCK IN when you need it.
