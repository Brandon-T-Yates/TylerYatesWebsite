Quick help

A few things to check.

Screen Time access

Unbound needs Screen Time authorization to apply app restrictions.

Changing allowed apps

Review your allowed apps from Unbound. While locked in, you may need to unlock before editing your selection.

Restrictions aren't behaving as expected

Check iOS Settings → Screen Time → Apps With Screen Time Access. The exact wording or location may vary by iOS version.

Removing Unbound

Unlock before deleting the app so your restrictions can be cleared properly.

When you contact support

Helpful details to include.

If you can, include a few details that may help diagnose the issue:

  • iPhone model
  • iOS version
  • Unbound version
  • a short description of what happened

Unbound does not collect or send these details automatically.
