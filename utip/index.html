uTip

A simpler way to tip.

Enter your bill, choose a tip percentage, and see the tip and total instantly. uTip keeps an everyday calculation quick and convenient, with whole-dollar rounding and display options that work for you.

Features

Adjustable tips
Choose the tip percentage that suits your bill.
Instant calculations
Your tip amount and total update automatically.
Whole-dollar rounding
Round the total up or down to the nearest whole dollar.
Light & dark modes
Choose the appearance you prefer.
Adjustable text size
Use larger text for easier reading.
Simple interface
A clear, easy-to-use layout for a quick calculation.

Product principles

Simple by design.

Local calculations · No account · Your display preferences

Tip calculations happen on your device. Bill amounts, tip percentages, and totals are not sent to external servers. Your dark-mode and larger-text preferences are stored locally, so the app stays convenient to use.
